NHS Rota Sync

NHS Rota Sync is a personal automation project that retrieves my NHS rota spreadsheets from Gmail, processes the rota information on my Raspberry Pi, and synchronises the resulting work commitments with my Google Calendar for display on a personal smart mirror.

The application is intended for my own use and is not offered as a public service.

Privacy

RotaSync accesses Gmail and Google Calendar only for the purposes of processing my rota and maintaining my calendar. Rota files and related processing data may be stored locally on my Raspberry Pi. Google services process data according to their own privacy policies.
